## When does this come up?

Cuba begins as an inactive Soviet ally — it shelters the Lourdes intelligence station, allows limited Soviet basing, and supports Cuban air units and a submarine. The NATO player may pay 6 OPS to attack and activate Cuba (5.4.4, 8.4.5). After the attack, Cuban Soviet forces become available to the Soviet player.

## Procedure

1. Spend 6 OPS (NATO only) and declare the attack on Cuba (5.4.4).
2. The Soviet player deploys the Cuban submarine: on a roll of 8+, deploy within one zone of Cuba; otherwise it begins in port. Deploy Cuban Air Units on Cuba with the SAM marker (8.4.5).
3. NATO may resolve up to 3 OPS worth of submarine or Task Force cruise missile attacks against Cuba — these are **free** (no additional OPS cost).
4. Place the four US units marked 'Cuba' in Florida and fly an immediate Strike mission with them against Cuba.
   {{PA-BLOCK: pa5:air-units-transit-to-a-zone — Air Units · Transit to a Zone}}
   {{PA-BLOCK: pa6:air-units-bombing-land-targets — Air Units Bombing Land Targets}}
   {{PA-BLOCK: pa6:sam-suppression-vs-bombing — SAM Suppression (SEAD) · vs Bombing}}
   {{PA-BLOCK: pa6:sam-attack-vs-bombing — SAM Attack · vs Bombing}}
   {{PA-BLOCK: pa6:bombing — Bombing (air-to-ground)}}
5. Resolve damage to the Lourdes Facility per 2.2.4.1 (loss of Lourdes triggers RORSAT consequences).
   {{PA-BLOCK: pa6:collateral-damage — Collateral Damage}}
6. Cuba is now active. The Soviet player may use Cuban forces as his own, and may immediately deploy up to three air units from any Soviet base to Cuba (all marked 'Spent').

## Why and what to watch for

Attacking Cuba is NATO's nuclear option against the Soviet Caribbean position, 6 OPS to neutralize the Lourdes intelligence station and the Cuban basing privileges (5.4.4, 8.4.5). The design models a strategic decision: NATO surrenders a major OPS commitment in exchange for permanently degrading Soviet intelligence and removing a forward basing option. After the attack, Cuba "activates", Soviet may now use Cuban forces freely and deploy up to 3 air units there.

Take this when the Lourdes destruction value exceeds the 6 OPS price plus the cost of activating Cuban Soviet forces. Lourdes destruction permanently removes 1 RORSAT and temporarily removes another for 3 turns (2.2.4.1), significant for Soviet detection. The 3 free OPS of submarine/TF cruise missiles get tacked on, plus a free Strike with the 4 Cuba-marked US units flown from Florida (5.4.4 step 3-4). In total NATO is firing ~5 OPS of value for 6 OPS spend, with the bonus of permanently damaging Soviet RORSAT capacity. Worth it only when Lourdes is producing real detection threats and NATO has the OPS slack.

The gotcha is the activation downside. Cuba activates immediately on the attack: Soviet now deploys the Cuban submarine (8+ deploys within one zone of Cuba, otherwise in port) and the Cuban Air Units with SAM marker. Cuba's MP could already operate while inactive (8.4.5 clarification), so MP doesn't change much, but Strike units do, Soviet can transfer up to 3 air units from any Soviet base to Cuba immediately (all Spent). Resolve the Strike's bombing carefully: SAM defends Lourdes, and collateral damage on Lourdes is what triggers the RORSAT loss (7.5.3, 2.2.4.1). And note 'kills' on the bombing table do the SAM-suppression work, total dice for SEAD allocation requires planning before the bomb roll.

This pairs with [Air Strike](./act-air-strike.md) procedure for the bombing resolution and [Launch RORSAT](./act-misc-launch-rorsat.md), knowing Lourdes is gone, Soviet must replace lost RORSATs through normal launches. Counter from Soviet: hold spy reserves to keep Lourdes-related cards live, and time Cuban activation to maximize the value of the activation deployment.

Example: NATO spends 6 OPS turn 4. Soviet deploys Cuban Foxtrot sub on 7 (port, failed deploy roll), Cuban MiG-21 on Cuba with SAM. NATO spends 3 free OPS on submarine cruise missiles against Cuba, 2 hits on the airfield. NATO flies in the 4 US Cuba units (A-7, F-4 escort, etc.), resolves SEAD, SAM attack, bombing, Lourdes takes 4 hits including 2 kills. Soviet selects RORSAT permanent removal (US rolled 5, didn't beat 6+, so Soviet selects), removes a worn RORSAT. Cuba is now active.
