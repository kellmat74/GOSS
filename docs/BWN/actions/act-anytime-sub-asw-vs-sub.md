## When does this come up?

An enemy submarine moves into or through a Sea Zone where you have a submarine On Patrol. The on-patrol sub can interrupt and attack the moving submarine with a standard ASW attack (5.2.3, 7.2). Unlike reacting against a moving Task Force, the sub stays On Patrol afterward — it is not marked Spent.

## Procedure

1. An enemy submarine moves into or through your On-Patrol sub's zone. Declare the reaction (5.2.3, 4.2.3.2).
2. Resolve a standard ASW attack: roll the sub's Anti-Submarine Warfare dice, add Tactical Value, apply DRMs (Bad Weather -1, Arctic ice -1, Hydrophones +1). Each die ≥ the target submarine's Defense value causes a step loss. (N) submarines are only killed on a natural 10 (7.2.1).
   {{PA-BLOCK: pa3:anti-submarine-warfare — Anti-Submarine Warfare (die roll table + DRMs)}}
3. Apply N10 effects (option to detect a TF in zone, destroy any sub step, etc.).
4. The defender applies non-N10 hits first, then the attacker allocates the rest. Apply sub 'save' rolls.
5. The On-Patrol sub remains On Patrol — reacting against a moving submarine does not flip it to Spent (5.2.3). It may react again later in the turn against another crossing.

## Coach

React whenever a target sub crosses your On-Patrol boat's zone and the math favors you, it costs nothing and the sub stays On Patrol afterward (5.2.3), so a good ambush spot can rack up multiple kills across a turn. (N) boats only die on a natural 10 (7.2.1). This is the sub-hunting mirror of the TF reaction, but the TF version flips the reactor to Spent (5.2.3) while this one does not.

## Why and what to watch for

An On-Patrol submarine reacting to an enemy submarine crossing into its zone lets a quiet boat ambush passing traffic without spending an action. The reaction uses the standard 7.2 ASW procedure and, per 5.2.3, does not mark the reacting sub Spent, only the Task Force-reaction version of On-Patrol duty does that. This makes a well-placed On-Patrol boat in a chokepoint valuable across an entire turn, not just once.

Take this whenever an enemy submarine moves into or through your On-Patrol sub's zone: roll ASW dice plus Tactical Value against the mover's Defense value, with the usual DRM stack (Bad Weather -1, Arctic -1, Hydrophones +1). (N) boats (Sierra, Akula) are only killed by a natural 10 (7.2.1). Because the sub stays On Patrol afterward, it remains available to react to the next submarine that crosses the same zone later in the turn, unlike the TF-reaction case, which flips to Spent and is done for the turn.

This pairs with [Hydrophone Barrier reaction](./act-anytime-hydrophone.md) firing first if the mover crossed a SOSUS line, and with [On-Patrol MP attacks a crossing submarine](./act-anytime-mp-vs-sub.md) when the on-patrol asset is air instead. Counter: route subs around known On-Patrol zones, or clear them with a strike first.

A NATO Los Angeles On Patrol in North 5-6 reacts to a Soviet Akula transiting through. ASW 3 dice + 2 Tactical, but the Akula is (N) so only a natural 10 hits. Rolls 10, 6, 4, natural 10 lands a step loss. The Los Angeles stays On Patrol, ready to react again if another Soviet boat follows.
